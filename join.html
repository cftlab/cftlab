Ph.D. and M.S. Positions

Open Positions in the CFT Lab

The Complex Flows and Advanced Transport Lab is looking for motivated graduate students interested in computational fluid dynamics, transport phenomena, scientific machine learning, and high-performance computing. Positions are available across all active research thrusts. Funded positions include tuition coverage and a competitive stipend.

High School Research Internships

The lab welcomes motivated high school students for research internships. Interns work alongside graduate students on active research projects, gaining hands-on experience in computational science and engineering research.
